Privacy Policy - Abhas Reel Scheduler

Last updated: 2026-07-26

Abhas Reel Scheduler is a personal-use application built and operated solely by its owner, Abhas Jain, to publish the owner's own video content to the owner's own Instagram and YouTube accounts. It is not distributed to, or used by, any other users.

What data this application accesses

This application requests the minimum permissions needed to upload videos on the owner's behalf:

  • YouTube: the youtube.upload scope, used only to publish video files the owner has created to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Instagram: content-publishing permissions, used only to publish video files the owner has created to the owner's own Instagram accounts.

What this application does not do

  • It does not access, read, or store any other users' data.
  • It does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Data storage

Access and refresh tokens are stored in encrypted secret stores (GitHub Actions Secrets and Cloudflare Workers KV) and are used only by this application's own automated posting pipeline.
